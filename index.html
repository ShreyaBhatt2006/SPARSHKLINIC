About The Sparsh Klinic

SPARSH KLINIC in Lucknow is an exclusive and unique hair transplant and cosmetic surgery centre, well equipped with modern instruments and facilities to ensure successful procedures. From reception to OT to resting rooms, every corner of the centre is cared for and sterilized,.. with facilities at par with international standards.Our work has earned us a reputation as the best hair transplant centre in North India, attracting patients from UAE, Saudi Arabia, U.S.A, Canada, U.K, Singapore, Australia, New Zealand, Bangladesh, Nepal and more.

Our center

The Sparsh Klinic is dedicated to helping patients feel confident with beautiful, healthy skin and hair. Our expert dermatologists and transplant specialists provide treatment plans that are safe, evidence-based, and tailored to each individual. .. The techniques we use to restore hair include Strip Technique (FUT) and FUE Technique, both designed for natural results and minimal scarring.

Our team

The hair transplant team at Sparsh Klinic is led by Dr. Ankit Kapoor (MBBS, MD) with more than 10 years of cosmetic and dermatological experience. ... Our surgeons and technicians have international exposure to the latest hair transplant and cosmetic surgery techniques, with awards, memberships, and research affiliations to their credit.

Facilities

Our centre is outfitted with world-class facilities from reception to OT. Operation theatres are sterilized before every surgery to maintain a hygienic, infection-free atmosphere. ... During procedures, patients may watch TV, films, or read books in full comfort, while the surgery remains completely painless. We also arrange food and accommodation for outstation patients on demand.
